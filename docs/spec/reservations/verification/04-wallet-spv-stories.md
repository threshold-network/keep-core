### Wallet operator (keep-core)

#### US-20 - Acceptance proposal: discover, sign, broadcast
- **Actor:** Wallet operator (keep-core tbtcpg) on the custodying Live/MovingFunds wallet.
- **Given/When/Then:** A `ReservationAcceptanceRequested` event exists for the operator's wallet with a `Pending` action. The operator runs `ReservationAcceptanceTask.Run`, which finds the candidate, validates the proposal against the snapshotted action record (age, refund margin, fee, amount, deposit-vault routing, deposit freshness), assembles the 1-in-1-out anchor transaction (`AssembleReservationAnchorTransaction` at `keep-core:pkg/tbtc/reservation.go:403`), and submits it for signing. After broadcasting, the SPV maintainer proves it.
- **Functions:** `keep-core:pkg/tbtcpg/reservation_acceptance.go:191` (`Run`), `:304` (`findReservationAcceptanceCandidates`), `:1148` (`proposeReservationAcceptance`); `keep-core:pkg/tbtc/reservation.go:403` (`AssembleReservationAnchorTransaction`), `:536` (`newReservationAnchorAction`); `tbtc-v2:solidity/contracts/bridge/WalletProposalValidator.sol:963` (`validateReservationAnchorProposal`).
- **Events:** Operator emits no on-chain event; the SPV maintainer emits `ReservationAccepted` (US-07) once the proof lands.
- **Reverts (validator, while signing):** "Reservations are disabled" `tbtc-v2:solidity/contracts/bridge/WalletProposalValidator.sol:971`; "Wallet is not in Live or MovingFunds state" `tbtc-v2:solidity/contracts/bridge/WalletProposalValidator.sol:1210`; "Acceptance action is not pending" `tbtc-v2:solidity/contracts/bridge/WalletProposalValidator.sol:994`; "Acceptance action has timed out" `tbtc-v2:solidity/contracts/bridge/WalletProposalValidator.sol:1002`; "Deposit min age not achieved yet" `tbtc-v2:solidity/contracts/bridge/WalletProposalValidator.sol:1018`; "Deposit not routed to the reservation vault" `tbtc-v2:solidity/contracts/bridge/WalletProposalValidator.sol:1030`; "Anchor amount below the reservation minimum" `tbtc-v2:solidity/contracts/bridge/WalletProposalValidator.sol:1050`; "Deposit refund safety margin is not preserved" `tbtc-v2:solidity/contracts/bridge/WalletProposalValidator.sol:1067`; "Deposit controlled by different wallet" `tbtc-v2:solidity/contracts/bridge/WalletProposalValidator.sol:1072`.

#### US-22 - Acceptance task: candidate inside timeout safety margin
