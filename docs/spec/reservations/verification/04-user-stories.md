### D28 keeper monitoring (implemented; live rollout pending)

#### US-100 Independent keeper alerts reach the existing monitoring receiver

- **Actor:** Operator of an independent existing `maintainer` instance and the monitoring service.
- **Given/When/Then:** A successful observation publishes four risk gauges and chain-head time. The configured monitoring service reads them. Every positive risk value produces its own event: late proof and no target are Critical; fee debt and unrequested-deposit deadline are Warning. A failed endpoint, invalid sample or stale observation produces a separate Critical health event. Fresh zero-risk samples produce no risk event.
- **Functions:** Observation and publication `keep-core@9c4e1b221:pkg/maintainer/spv/reservation_keeper_observer.go:98-190`; monitor `tbtc-v2@8eb26c2e2:monitoring/src/reservation-keeper-monitor.ts:90-228`; Sentry receiver `tbtc-v2@8eb26c2e2:monitoring/src/sentry-receiver.ts:26-69`.
- **Prerequisites and limits:** `RESERVATION_KEEPER_METRICS_URL` enables the monitor; `SENTRY_DSN` enables its receiver (`tbtc-v2@8eb26c2e2:monitoring/src/index.ts:70-100`). Operators must use the same network and the released deployment bindings/block. The observer sends no transactions or Bitcoin signatures. Its surrounding proof submitter still requires SPV authorization. Local receiver delivery does not establish live Sepolia rollout.
- **Test:** Healthy control and four alert classifications `tbtc-v2@8eb26c2e2:monitoring/test/reservation-keeper-monitor.test.ts:111-192`; unavailable endpoint and Manager delivery `tbtc-v2@8eb26c2e2:monitoring/test/reservation-keeper-monitor.test.ts:341-395`.
- **Rehearsed:** Actual keeper, normal monitor CLI and local Sentry transport, with controls and per-stage build limits (`keeper-alert-acceptance.md:6-37`, `keeper-alert-acceptance.md:92-141`). See [approved design deltas](13-approved-design-deltas.md).
- **Status:** holds for implementation and recorded local acceptance. Live operator configuration and delivery remain unproven.

### D26 release to pool (implemented)

#### US-90 - Release a reserved deposit back to the pool (depositor gets ordinary sweep / TBTC)
- **Actor:** Depositor.
- **Given/When/Then:** A reserved deposit is revealed, no acceptance request was ever made for it (`reservations[depositKey].requestNonce == 0`), its snapshotted refund deadline still leaves the 24-hour `DEPOSIT_REFUND_SAFETY_MARGIN` open, and the target is the zero address or a trusted vault that is not the reservation vault. The depositor calls `releaseReservedDepositToPool(depositKey, vault)`. **Then** the `pendingReservedDeposit` record is fully cleared (all four fields zeroed), `pendingReservedDeposits` decrements, `deposits[depositKey].vault` is re-routed to `vault`, and `ReservedDepositReleasedToPool(depositKey, depositor, vault)` is emitted. An ordinary sweep may now settle the deposit: the treasury fee recorded at reveal is charged by the sweep (the release itself applies no rebate), and the sats credit the chosen vault through the Bank, or the depositor's Bank balance directly when `vault` is the zero address.
- **Functions:** `tbtc-v2@d20cf74ba:solidity/contracts/bridge/ReservationRouter.sol:433` (`releaseReservedDepositToPool`, no caller gate besides the depositor check inside) -> `tbtc-v2@d20cf74ba:solidity/contracts/bridge/Reservation.sol:1373-1453`; interface at `tbtc-v2@d20cf74ba:solidity/contracts/bridge/IReservationBridge.sol:526`. Effects: field clears and `pendingReservedDeposits -= 1` at `tbtc-v2@d20cf74ba:solidity/contracts/bridge/Reservation.sol:1434-1438`, vault re-route at `tbtc-v2@d20cf74ba:solidity/contracts/bridge/Reservation.sol:1439`.
- **Events:** `ReservedDepositReleasedToPool(depositKey, depositor, vault)` declared at `Reservation.sol:423`, emitted at `:1448`; `DepositsSwept` after the subsequent ordinary sweep.
- **keep-core half (depositor gets TBTC):** At `keep-core@f99ef96e3` the ordinary deposit-sweep task skipped a deposit only when the reveal event's vault matched the live reservation vault AND `chain.IsReservedDeposit` (`keep-core:pkg/chain/ethereum/tbtc.go:1833`, reading `pendingReservedDeposit[key].isReserved` at `Bridge.sol:1634`) returned true. After a release, `isReserved` is false, so `findDeposits` did NOT skip the deposit and the sweep grouped it by the live `depositRequest.Vault` (`keep-core:pkg/tbtcpg/deposit_sweep.go:333`, `:398-438`) - the re-routed vault or zero - producing an ordinary sweep proposal. That read path was correct by reading but had no dedicated keep-core test at `f99ef96e3` (`holds-untested`). The post-release sweep is pinned at `keep-core@4b95f0a06` (release binding `8a714f2c1`, sweep tests `4b95f0a06`), which `f99ef96e3` predates.
- **Test:** `tbtc-v2@d20cf74ba:solidity/test/bridge/Bridge.ReservationReleaseToPool.test.ts:350` ("releases to a trusted vault, an ordinary sweep proof succeeds and credits the vault through the Bank"), `tbtc-v2@d20cf74ba:solidity/test/bridge/Bridge.ReservationReleaseToPool.test.ts:407` ("releases to the zero address and an ordinary sweep credits the depositor Bank balance"), `tbtc-v2@d20cf74ba:solidity/test/bridge/Bridge.ReservationReleaseToPool.test.ts:434` ("lets governance re-point the reservation vault once the last pending reserved deposit is released") - all at `tbtc-v2@d20cf74ba`.
- **Status:** `holds` (on-chain, at d20cf74ba); keep-core sweep half `holds` at `4b95f0a06` (`TestFindDepositsToSweep_IncludesReleasedDeposit`, `TestValidateDepositSweepProposal_AcceptsReleasedDeposit`), `holds-untested` at `f99ef96e3` (no dedicated test). The traceability row records the most cautious of the three, `holds-untested`, so its grade comes from the `f99ef96e3` keep-core half and not from the on-chain half, which is `holds` at `d20cf74ba`.

## Findings

- `ReservationVault.receiveBalanceIncrease` (`tbtc-v2:solidity/contracts/vault/ReservationVault.sol:161`) trusts every Bank-routed credit unconditionally: the function has no `reservationKey` parameter and cannot verify the credit corresponds to a Bridge-proven reservation anchor. The deploy pipeline marks the vault `isVaultTrusted` only after it is wired into the Bridge on non-local networks, so the scripted activation path cannot trigger this gap. That ordering is enforced by the deploy script (`deploy/97_set_reservation_parameters.ts`), not by an on-chain check: governance retains the raw ability to call `setVaultStatus(vault, true)` directly through `BridgeGovernance` (`BridgeGovernance.sol:308`), bypassing the script's precondition. Documented as KNOWN GAP at `ReservationVault.sol:147-160`. Severity: medium for mainnet. Reproduction idea: governance executes `setVaultStatus` on a vault that has not been wired into the Bridge; a non-reservation Bank credit routed to that vault (e.g. a future code path) would mint TBTC against no proven anchor.

- Negative tests for `notifyReservationAcceptanceTimedOut` ("Action type mismatch", "Action is not pending", "Action has not timed out") are not directly pinned in the Solidity test suite. The internal guards at `Reservation.sol:681-690` are correct, and the function is exercised positively in `Reservation.test.ts:1492` and `tbtc-v2:solidity/test/bridge/Bridge.ReservationOccupancy.test.ts:68`, but the per-guard reverts are inferred rather than asserted. Status in the main file: US-06 marks them `holds-untested`.

- "Late acceptance settlement window expired" (`ReservationProofs.sol:210`) is not asserted in any test. The bounded late window is exercised end-to-end by `test/bridge/ReservationProofs.test.ts:851` ("should settle a timed-out older generation without newer generation (late settlement)"), but the negative expire boundary is not. Status in the main file: US-08 marks it `holds-untested`.

- "Anchor below reservation minimum" (`ReservationProofs.sol:465`) and "Re-anchor amount below the dust floor" (`ReservationProofs.sol:697`) are not directly asserted in tests. The request-time checks (`Reservation.sol:560-562`; `:854-857`) make them unreachable in normal flow, but the on-chain guards remain. Status in the main file: US-45 marks them `holds-untested`.

- "Caller is not SPV maintainer" (`ReservationRouter.sol:198`) is not directly asserted at the router entry. The proof tests in `ReservationProofs.test.ts` exercise the modifier implicitly by using a maintainer signer; the revert is not asserted at the router boundary. Status in the main file: US-42 marks it `holds-untested`.

- The on-chain `forceStaleReservedDeposit` and `setSpvMaintainerStatus` paths have no dedicated Solidity tests. `forceStaleReservedDeposit` is exercised only as setup in `tbtc-v2@4c98e6b82:solidity/test/bridge/Bridge.ReservationReleaseToPool.test.ts:584-586`, through the router ABI attached to the Bridge: a governance call marks the deposit stale and the following release reverts "Not a pending reserved deposit", so neither its guards nor its counter and event are asserted. The underlying `Reservation.forceStaleReservedDeposit` shares the same guards as the permissionless `notifyStaleReservedDeposit` (which is fully tested), and its delay-free `onlyOwner` wrapper is documented at `tbtc-v2@4c98e6b82:solidity/contracts/bridge/BridgeGovernance.sol:1939-1941`. `setSpvMaintainerStatus` similarly has no test, though the keep-core tests assume the maintainer status is set. Status: US-65 and the coverage-table row for `setSpvMaintainerStatus` are `holds-untested`.

- The C-4 ordering gate (`setVaultStatus(vault, true)` at or after the keep-core activation block B) is enforced operationally by the deploy script `tbtc-v2:solidity/deploy/97_set_reservation_parameters.ts` and documented at `tbtc-v2:solidity/docs/RESERVATION_CAPS_DEPLOYMENT.md:80-96`, not by an on-chain require. The on-chain code is silent on activation ordering; an on-chain check would require a cross-network lookup, which the architecture deliberately avoids. Status: US-62 marks the on-chain code `holds` and the operational gate `holds-untested`.
